# Privacy Policy

## Child Safety Standards

### Our commitment
FlipStar is committed to protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward any content that sexually exploits or endangers minors.

### Content moderation
All user-generated content on FlipStar is subject to automated and manual moderation review. Any content that depicts, promotes, or facilitates child sexual abuse material (CSAM) is immediately removed and reported to the relevant authorities.

### Prohibited content
The following is strictly prohibited on FlipStar:

- Any content that sexually exploits minors
- Any content that depicts child nudity or sexual abuse
- Any communication that facilitates grooming, exploitation, or abuse of minors

### Reporting
Users can report child safety concerns directly in the app via the Report button on any Flip or user profile. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team within 24 hours.

To report a child safety concern outside the app, contact us at: support@skykintech.com.et

### Compliance
FlipStar complies with all applicable Ethiopian laws regarding child protection and reports confirmed CSAM to the relevant Ethiopian national authorities.
